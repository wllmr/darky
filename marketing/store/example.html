Walking the coastal path

The path starts at the harbor and follows the shoreline north. Most of the route is flat, with a few short climbs above the rocks. Allow an afternoon for the walk and the return trip.

Beyond the lighthouse, the trail turns inland through a stretch of pine forest. There is a small beach at the next bay, a good place to stop before heading back.
